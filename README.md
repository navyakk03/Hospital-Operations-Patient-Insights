# Hospital Operations & Patient Insights Dashboard

📌 Executive Summary

This project presents an end-to-end data analysis of hospital operations focused on patient volume, length of stay, and treatment cost patterns. Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, the analysis transforms raw hospital data into an interactive dashboard that highlights key operational metrics and supports data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Business Problem

Healthcare organizations require clear visibility into patient flow, length of stay, and cost distribution to improve operational efficiency and resource planning. Without consolidated insights, identifying high-cost drivers and patient stay patterns becomes challenging.
This project addresses these challenges by analyzing hospital patient data and visualizing key operational indicators through an interactive dashboard.

🛠️ Methodology

1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python)
   
     Date formatting and validation
   
     Handling missing and inconsistent values
   
     Feature preparation such as age group and stay category
   
2. Data Analysis (SQL)
   
     Aggregation of patient counts and costs
   
     Year-wise and category-wise analysis
   
3.Visualization & Reporting (Power BI)
     
   KPI cards for total patients, total treatment cost, and average length of stay
     
   Interactive slicers for age group and admission year
     
   Charts for trend analysis, cost distribution, and patient segmentation

📊 Key Insights / Results

The dashboard shows 984 total patients with an overall treatment cost of ~8M
Long-stay patients contribute the highest share of total treatment cost
Elderly patients have a higher average length of stay compared to adults
Patient admissions vary across years, with a noticeable decline in recent years
Gender-wise distribution of patients is nearly balanced
Average length of stay (~37.66 days) is a critical metric influencing overall cost

✅ Recommendations

Closely monitor long-stay patients to control extended hospitalization costs
Improve discharge planning, especially for elderly patients
Track length of stay as a KPI to support operational and capacity planning
Use year-wise trends to anticipate patient volume changes
